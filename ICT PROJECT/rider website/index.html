Reitwagen: (First Motorcycle)

The invention of the motorcycle is generally credited to Gottlieb Daimler and Wilhelm Maybach in 1885. They built the first true motorcycle, called the Reitwagen, in Germany. This vehicle was powered by a gasoline engine and featured two wheels, making it the first motorized two-wheeled vehicle that resembled the motorcycles we know today.

    TRIUMOH

    Founded in 1902, Triumph Motorcycles, a British legend, specializes in blending classic designs with modern performance. It offers a diverse range, from roadsters to adventure bikes.

    SUZUKI

    Established in 1909, Suzuki, a Japanese brand, delivers versatile motorcycles ranging from sport bikes to cruisers. It's recognized for reliability, efficiency, and advanced engineering at competitive prices.

    KTM

    KTM is an Austrian motorcycle, bicycle and motorsports brand which is co-owned by Indian manufacturer Bajaj Auto and Austrian manufacturer Pierer Mobility AG. It traces its foundation in 1934 as Kronreif & Trunkenpolz Mattighofen.

    DUCATI

    Founded in 1926, Ducati, an Italian brand, is synonymous with premium motorcycles combining style, power, and performance. It stands out with its racing heritage and iconic V-twin engines.

    BMW

    Founded in 1916, BMW Motorrad produces luxury motorcycles offering exceptional engineering, design, and performance. Known for innovation, it caters to both touring and sporty riding experiences.
